# Signal Log

## Signal Classification Guide

- Signal Layer: `Leading`, `Validation`, `Lagging`
- Claim Type: `Verified Fact`, `Management Claim`, `Market Inference`, `Speculation To Verify`, `Action Language To Ignore`

Every row must follow `Evidence → Thesis Impact → Next Verification`.
